IdentiDeform V3 contact review (round 3)

72 validation samples, two full action cycles, final setup only (three camera views per clip). Objects come from the screened V3 split, so they differ from rounds 1–2. Object screening · round 2 · round 1

  • Everything acts by contact: plates and grippers start outside the object; nothing holds the object's own particles at t = 0.
  • Support bed (press, press–hold–release, shear): for every floor column the support rises to just below the object's lowest point in that column, so legs, rims and overhangs are carried and the object cannot tip or sink before the press.
  • Press plate is a one-sided rigid plate that lifts back at a finite speed instead of vanishing, so springy objects follow it back and are not launched.
  • Grippers (shear, stretch, bend) are sized to each end's local cross-section. Stretch has one fixed and one moving gripper, bend clamps both ends, shear holds the base on a sticky bed, so the object neither translates nor rotates as a whole. If an object's ends along its longest axis are too thin to grip (skeleton), the grippers use the longer horizontal axis.
  • Gravity: g = 2 for press, press–hold–release and drop; 0 otherwise. Ranges: log10E/ρ jelly 3.5–6, plasticine 3.5–5.5, metal 4.5–6; log10σy/ρ 3.2–4.5 for plasticine and metal, so the yield stress carries the object's own weight at g = 2.
  • Objects: the 31 objects marked bad or unsure are excluded; val and test were refilled to 10 objects each from good train objects (train 57).
Open points: (1) stretch still tears in 4/12 samples (thin walls); I would lower the pull from 20% to 15%. (2) Thin standing objects (a wall on its edge) buckle and tip over under the press, which is physically reasonable. (3) The hamburger needs a new 3D asset (image-to-3D generator, needs your approval); it is excluded for now. If this looks right I start the full regeneration.

Press

Rigid frictionless plate pushes down 70% of the height over 3 s, then lifts back over 1 s (only pushes, never pulls). Support bed shaped to the underside; g = 2. Broken into pieces: 0/12.

Press–hold–release

Same plate: 30% in 0.5 s, hold to 1.0 s, lift back over 0.5 s, then free until 4 s. g = 2. Broken into pieces: 0/12.

Shear

Underside held by a sticky support bed; a gripper closes on the top 20% (0–0.5 s), holds, then drags it sideways by 25% of the object extent; opens at 3 s. g = 0. Broken into pieces: 0/12.

Stretch

One gripper holds one end, a second gripper pulls the other end out by 20% of the length (tensile-test style). g = 0. Broken into pieces: 4/12.

Bend

Both ends clamped by fixed grippers; a plate pushes the middle sideways by 35% of the thickness. g = 0. Broken into pieces: 0/12.

Drop

Free fall from 0.25 above the floor under g = 2 (impact speed about 1.0). Broken into pieces: 0/12.
