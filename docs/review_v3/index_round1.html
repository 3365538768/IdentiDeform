IdentiDeform V3 contact review

36 validation samples (one full action cycle over objects and materials). Each clip has three rows, each with three camera views: old V2, V3 with gravity 9.8, and V3 with gravity 0. Parameters, objects and cameras are identical across rows.

  • Plates now act by contact from outside the object, as in the gripper task. The press plate starts above the object instead of inside it.
  • Shear: a grip holds the top 15% of the object and drags it sideways while a sticky floor holds the base. Stretch: grips hold the outer 8% of both ends and pull apart. Bend: three-point bend with a fixed support in the middle of one side and two pushers at the ends of the other.
  • With gravity 9.8 every object also rests on a floor. Many objects with low E/ρ or low yield stress still slump into a puddle under their own weight within 0.5 s, so the action barely matters.
  • With gravity 0 the objects stay intact and the action is what you see. Drop starts with the same impact speed as a 0.25 fall under gravity. The identifiable quantities stay E/ρ and σy/ρ.
  • "Pieces" is the largest share of the object's silhouette that is separated from the main body in any frame. Red means more than 1%.
Decision needed before the full run: which row should the full regeneration use? My recommendation is gravity 0. Samples #10 and #26 failed to simulate with gravity 0 and are shown without that row.
