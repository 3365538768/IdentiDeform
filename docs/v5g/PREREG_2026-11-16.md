# V5g pre-registration: twin-planned grasp-and-lift (grasp POSITION x grip LEVEL), two-sided failure (2026-11-16 07:07 UTC)

Written after a GT-only pilot (6 V5b val samples: 2, 5, 21, 27, 31, 37; data/v5g/pilot, data/v5g/pilot2) and BEFORE any twin
simulation. The pilot samples are part of the evaluation set (their GT outcomes were seen while setting levels and thresholds).

## Task (scripts/v5g/grasp_main.py, grasp_run.py)
- Object (V5b val, the same 60 as v5r: docs/v5r/SAMPLES.json) rests on the V3 support bed + a slip floor; gravity g = 2 (as V5b).
- Two rigid sticky jaws (geometry of scripts/v2/contact_bc._gripper) on a segment of 25 % of the long horizontal axis, closing along
  the other horizontal axis over 0-0.4 s by depth = level x local thickness per jaw (floor 1 grid cell); bed removed at 0.6 s; lift
  by 30 % of the object height over 0.6-1.0 s; hold to 1.6 s; lower back over 1.6-2.0 s; jaws open at 2.1 s; relax on the floor to 2.6 s.
- Candidates: position in {end (outer 25 %), centre (middle 25 %)} x level in {0.03, 0.06, 0.10, 0.16, 0.25, 0.40} = 12, plus one
  no-jaw reference run (same parameters) for the damage metric.

## Outcome of a simulated candidate
- FAIL-drop: left > 0.5, where left = fraction of particles that rose less than half of the jaw lift, averaged over the hold window
  1.3-1.6 s (most of the object is not carried: slip, tear, droop or rotation out of the grip). A failed simulation counts as FAIL-drop.
- FAIL-damage (if not dropped): |crush| > 5 %, crush = permanent change of the jaw-segment width along the jaw axis after release
  (whole object Procrustes-aligned to the no-jaw reference run, mean of the last 0.2 s, divided by the segment thickness).
  5 % instead of the suggested 3 %: at MPM resolution the gentlest admissible grip (1 cell per jaw) already leaves 1-4 % on the stiff
  pilot objects, so 3 % would label almost every grasp of a stiff object as damaging.
- SUCCESS otherwise. Grip force = mean jaw-axis force of the two jaws on the object over the hold window (GT units).

## Planner, arms, metrics
- In each twin, simulate all 12 candidates and choose, among predicted successes, the LOWEST level (gentlest); ties -> lower
  predicted left (less sag), then centre; none -> ABSTAIN. Execute with GT parameters. Success = GT success at the chosen grasp, or
  ABSTAIN when no candidate is GT-feasible. Oracle = the planner applied to GT.
- Arms: GT oracle; (b) vision prior twin (V5b 9-run ensemble); (c) vision + touch + action prior twin (docs/v5m); (a) class-mean twin
  (V5b train mean of the vision-predicted law); (d) fixed grasp = one candidate or ABSTAIN for every object, tuned leave-one-out on
  the GT outcomes of the other samples (max success, ties -> gentler). Twins: density 1000 + specific moduli, hardening/softening
  0.25, nu from each prior (scripts/v5r/twins.py, unchanged).
- Metrics: success, drop rate, damage rate, missed grasp (abstain although feasible), level regret |idx(chosen) - idx(oracle)|,
  relative force regret (F_GT(chosen) - F_GT(oracle)) / F_GT(oracle) on successful grasps; paired contrasts (c)-(b), (b)-(a),
  twins - fixed; object bootstrap, 2000 draws, 95 % percentile CIs (scripts/v5g/plan_eval.py). Sensitivity: T_drop 0.4 / 0.6,
  T_damage 3 % / 8 %.
- Compute: a800-4 only (each sample on one host). If time runs out (deadline 10:40 UTC), report the samples complete for all arms.
